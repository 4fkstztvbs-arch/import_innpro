Creality CR-Silk PLA Filament (Purple)

Creality's CR-Silk filament features a metallic, elegant sheen, making it eye-catching and perfect for printing figurines, decorations and even jewelry. It also features impressive durability and compatibility with most Creality printers. What's more, durable yet environmentally friendly materials have been used to make it.

Fast and trouble-free printing

Thanks to its careful winding, the filament allows for smooth, trouble-free printing at speeds of up to 100 mm/s. It is also resistant to tangling. This will save you time and avoid wasting materials!

Elegant and durable

The filament is very pleasant to the touch and is distinguished by its elegant, delicate sheen - it allows you to achieve a pearly and smooth surface of the model without additional polishing. In addition, it was made of environmentally friendly, durable materials (PLA), making it resistant to deformation and distinguished by low shrinkage. Its tensile strength reaches 35 MPa, and its flexural strength is as high as 67 MPa.
