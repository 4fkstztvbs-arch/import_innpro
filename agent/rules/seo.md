# SEO rules

Monitor:
- titles
- meta descriptions
- H1
- product names
- category content
- internal linking
- canonicals
- structured data
- sitemaps
- indexation
- 404s
- redirect chains
- duplicate/thin content
- Search Console queries and landing pages

High-priority opportunities:
- high impressions
- positions roughly 4-20
- below-expected CTR
- strong traffic but weak conversion

## Protected SEO elements
Do not change without explicit approval:
- URLs
- global canonical logic
- robots.txt
- large-scale redirects

Do not infer SEO impact from correlation alone. Prefer before/after measurement with suitable controls where possible.

## Product and seasonal content

Product-page rewrites, internal linking, verified accessory/cross-sell links, category-copy improvement and seasonal merchandising are governed by:
- `product-content-seo.md`
- `../runbooks/product-content-seo.md`

Thin content is an ongoing backlog signal, not merely an SEO warning.

Prefer durable improvements that survive supplier imports. Preserve established seasonal URLs when they remain useful; do not recreate the same seasonal category every year merely for freshness.


## Approved optimization reference

For product/category naming, search-intent targeting, content structure and internal linking, follow `approved-optimization-framework.md` before generic SEO best practice.

Product and category names should be informed by how people actually search when reliable query/demand data exists. Preserve protected URLs unless separately approved.

The current shared Kvasnička document is an approved project reference for landing-page quality, content structure, internal linking and design/CRO direction.
