# Future agent capabilities — public research notes (2026-09)

**Status:** REFERENCE ONLY / NOT ACTIVE AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES

This document preserves ideas found in recent public e-commerce / retail AI webinars and case-study material for possible future PremiumStore expansion.

These ideas must **not** become production responsibilities merely because they are listed here. Any future activation requires the same PremiumStore governance path:
`OBSERVE -> SHADOW -> PILOT -> CONTROLLED AUTONOMY`.

## Why this file exists

PremiumStore already has a broad internal AI e-commerce manager covering:
- business/performance analytics
- products and assortment
- SEO/content/UX/design
- merchandising
- Heureka/acquisition
- feed/distribution
- QA/technical operations
- experiments/reporting
- internal search
- seasonal planning
- competitive context

Recent public material suggests several additional directions that may become valuable later.

## Future idea 1 — Customer-facing Product Advisor

A conversational shopping assistant directly on PremiumStore that:
- understands catalogue/product parameters
- asks clarifying questions
- recommends 2-3 suitable products
- explains differences
- suggests compatible accessories
- helps customers choose without forcing them to understand supplier terminology

Potential value:
- higher product discovery quality
- better conversion on complex technical categories
- fewer pre-sale support questions
- stronger cross-sell

Important guardrails:
- never invent compatibility or technical claims
- recommendations must use verified catalogue data
- clear distinction between advice and factual product data

Public research direction:
- Constructor: Product Insights Agent / proactive shopping guidance / predictive intent
- Adobe Commerce: conversational commerce
- Salesforce: agentic shopping

## Future idea 2 — AI / GEO Discovery Manager

Expand Feed & Distribution Manager into monitoring how PremiumStore products are understood and surfaced by AI shopping/discovery systems.

Possible scope:
- Google AI Mode / AI Overviews / Gemini shopping experiences
- ChatGPT / OpenAI product discovery
- other AI shopping assistants
- structured product data readiness

Questions to monitor:
- Does the AI understand what the product is?
- Is price/availability correct?
- Are identifiers and attributes complete?
- Are accessories/alternatives understandable?
- Is PremiumStore discoverable for relevant purchase-intent prompts?

This should build on the current Feed & Distribution Manager rather than become a separate source of commercial truth.

## Future idea 3 — Predictive customer intent

Move from reacting only to explicit search queries toward inferring probable customer intent from behaviour such as:
- viewed category
- filters used
- product comparisons
- repeated visits
- viewed specifications
- cart context

Potential uses:
- better product recommendations
- dynamic merchandising
- relevant accessories
- better search suggestions
- contextual buying guidance

This requires strong privacy, measurement and experimentation guardrails.

## Future idea 4 — Proactive customer service

Potential future scope:
- order-status questions
- product FAQ
- tracking
- compatibility questions
- common pre-sale questions

Do not automatically include refunds, complaints, warranty decisions or unusual cases in autonomous scope.

## Future idea 5 — Voice of Customer / Customer Intelligence

Collect recurring customer questions and pain points from:
- support
- reviews
- returns
- reklamácie
- ad comments
- search queries

Then feed them into:
- Product Content Backlog
- FAQ/category content
- Content Gap Agent
- Internal Search
- Bundles/accessories
- QA

This can be very valuable once the required customer-support data source is connected.

## Future idea 6 — Retention / CRM Manager

Possible later role:
- customer segmentation
- abandoned-cart analysis
- post-purchase communication
- replenishment reminders
- win-back campaigns
- personalised email/SMS flows

This is intentionally not active today.

## Future idea 7 — Fulfillment / shipment exception manager

Potential monitoring:
- delayed parcels
- missing carrier scans
- failed delivery
- address issues
- unusually long fulfillment time

Objective:
detect a problem before the customer needs to ask.

This is operationally separate from the current growth agent and should only be added if the underlying carrier/order data becomes reliable.

## Research themes that validated the current PremiumStore direction

Public material consistently points toward:
- AI as an always-on analyst/category-manager assistant
- merchandising/search/product discovery automation
- catalogue enrichment and feed quality
- LLM/AI-shopping discoverability
- proactive anomaly detection
- experiment-led CRO
- agent workflows connected to commerce APIs rather than isolated chatbots
- staged autonomy with governance and human control over higher-risk writes
